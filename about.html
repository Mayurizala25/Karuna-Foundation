Our Story

Our journey is built on care.

Service to Animals is Service to God

Guided by the belief that "Service to Animal is Service to God," our journey began with the generous donation of a mobile ambulance van by the respected Kanabar family of Manavadar. This noble act inspired a wave of support from kind-hearted individuals and organizations, including Manishbhai Mansata, Shri Andh Apang Gausewa Trust, Mr. Madhoobhai Mehta, Meenalben Mehta (The Romeera Foundation, UK), and others. Their contributions of ambulances and food service vehicles have helped us reach and treat countless stray and injured animals across Gujarat.

Our Commitment

Three promises. One purpose.

01

Vision

To create a compassionate society where kindness towards animals is a core value. We believe that serving animals is a form of serving God. Our mission is to ensure every sick, injured, or stray animal receives the care they deserve.

02

Mission

To provide timely care to stray and sick animals through mobile veterinary services and shelters, promote compassion, vegetarianism, and non-violence, support gaushalas, and raise awareness to protect animals from cruelty and slaughter.

03

Core Purpose

Driven by the belief that "Service to Animals is Service to God," we rescue, treat, protect, and care for stray and injured animals. Our mission is to give every voiceless life the compassion, dignity, and care it deserves.
